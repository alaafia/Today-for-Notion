1. Connect Notion

  1. Open notion.so/profile/integrations and create an internal integration.
  2. Copy its Internal Integration Secret and paste it below.
  3. In Notion, open each database you want to use, click •••, then Connections, and add your integration.

2. Databases

Day headers in pages: each row is a page (a project, a plan) with a date range. Inside, lines like Wed 18, Tue 1 Dec or 2026-11-18 are followed by checkboxes. The popup shows the checkboxes under today's line.
Task rows: each row is a task. The popup shows rows whose date is today.
